Last reviewed · Sep 2026

“Safe” means the cited reference supports the plant as non-toxic or commonly used as forage; it does not set a portion size. Introduce any new food slowly and avoid sprayed or contaminated plants.

Plant identification is the first safety step. Common names overlap. If a plant is uncertain, treat it as unknown. For suspected poisoning, contact your veterinarian or a poison-control service instead of waiting for symptoms.
